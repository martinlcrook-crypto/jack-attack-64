HOW TO PLAY JACK ATTACK

GOAL: Defeat every monster on screen to clear the level!
3 WAYS TO DEFEAT MONSTERS:
1. STOMP them by jumping & landing on their heads.
2. SQUISH them sideways by pushing a block against a wall, bar, or another block.
3. CRUSH them by dropping a block onto them from above!
BLOCKS & JUMPING:
• Jack can jump up to 3 blocks high.
• Hold GRAB (Space/Shift/Z) + ←/→ to push (1 or more blocks) or pull (1 block).
• Watch out! Monsters & falling blocks can only kill Jack from above (side & underneath bumps are safe!).
WATER & BONUS BARS:
• Monsters float, Jack doesn't! Push blocks into water to build safe bridges.
• Jump on every glowing bar before the bonus timer runs out for huge bonus points — bars dissolve when time hits zero!
• Extra life every 20,000 points.
